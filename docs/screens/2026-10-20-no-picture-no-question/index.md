# No picture, no question

A question that can only be answered by looking at a picture is not served without one. Every template now declares `needsPicture`. If the picture fails to render or fails the picture check, another question from the same skill is served, or a plain ladder question.

- `angle-name.png` — "Is this angle right, acute or obtuse?" with both arms and a corner arc. Choices: right, acute, obtuse.
- `tape-where.png` — "Where is 3/4 on the tape?" Tape in equal parts, marks A, B, C. Choices are the letters.
- `tape-mark.png` — Reverse: one marked point, choices are fractions.
- `tape-length.png` — "2.8 ft on the tape" with the tape and a number box.
- `shape-name.png` — "What is this shape?" Drawn square. Choices: circle, triangle, square.
- `shape-corners.png` — "How many corners does this shape have?" Drawn triangle. Answer in the number box.
- `part-colored.png` — "What part of this plank is colored?" Two of three parts marked.
- `line-slope.png` — "What is the slope of this line?" Drawn line, answer in the number box.
- `assemble-tape.png` — Assemble: "It runs from 0 ft to 4 ft on the tape. How long?" The tape stays. The length goes in the number box.
