# Check and sketch

Each question uses the layout for its answer type. Check stays off until there is an answer. Enter checks too. Levels 1 and 2 use a green check mark with no word.

- `typed.png` — 1 + 6 + 9 = ? drawn inside the sketch. Check is disabled until a number is entered.
- `stacked.png` — 633,647 + 363,599 in stacked form inside the sketch, with room to write a carry.
- `choice.png` — Is 30 even or odd? Full width, no sketch. Check stays off until odd or even is picked.
- `picture-tap.png` — Tap the plank cut in half. The three cut planks are the choices. No extra plank above them. The button is a check mark.
- `drag.png` — Drag 6 bolts. One numeral, 6, with its dots, and one empty box. The button is a check mark.
- `kit-panel.png` — Kit cards grow to fit their text. Bottom beam stays inside its own card.
