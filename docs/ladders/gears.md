# gears ladder

## Level 1: Carousel

Job: Turn the horses. Output: horses.

Count the teeth on one gear, put it on, and crank until the horses go round.

Dials: numbers 0, representation 0, given 0, freedom 0, precision 0.

- 6 teeth and 8 teeth. Measure 6. Part 8. Predict 1. Output horses.
- 8 teeth and 10 teeth. Measure 8. Part 10. Predict 1. Output horses.
- 10 teeth and 8 teeth. Measure 10. Part 8. Predict 0. Output horses.

## Level 2: Carousel

Job: Count the turns. Output: horses.

Count to 20, see which gear has more teeth, and watch which way the second gear turns.

Dials: numbers 1, representation 0, given 1, freedom 0, precision 0.

- 16 driving 8. Measure 16. Part 8. Predict 0. Output horses.
- 20 driving 10. Measure 20. Part 10. Predict 2. Output horses.
- 18 driving 12. Measure 18. Part 12. Predict 1. Output horses.

## Level 3: Music box

Job: Pass the mark. Output: drum.

Turn the music box drum and count how many teeth pass the mark.

Dials: numbers 1, representation 1, given 1, freedom 1, precision 1.

- 12 teeth, 2 turns. Measure 12. Part 12. Predict 24. Output drum.
- 10 teeth, 3 turns. Measure 10. Part 10. Predict 30. Output drum.
- 8 teeth, even or odd. Measure 8. Part 8. Predict 1. Output drum.

## Level 4: Well winch

Job: Lift the bucket. Output: bucket.

Lift the 20 kg bucket out of the well with a crank a kid can turn.

Dials: numbers 1, representation 1, given 2, freedom 1, precision 1.

- 36 divided by 12. Measure 12. Part 36. Predict 3. Output bucket.
- 32 divided by 8. Measure 8. Part 32. Predict 4. Output bucket.
- 20 divided by 10. Measure 10. Part 20. Predict 2. Output bucket.

## Level 5: Well winch

Job: Whole ratios. Output: bucket.

Pick a gear pair that lifts the bucket by a whole number of turns, with whole spacing.

Dials: numbers 1, representation 1, given 2, freedom 1, precision 1.

- 12 and 24. Measure 12. Part 24. Predict 18. Output bucket.
- 8 and 16. Measure 8. Part 16. Predict 12. Output bucket.
- 9 and 27. Measure 9. Part 27. Predict 18. Output bucket.

## Level 6: Well winch

Job: A fraction of a turn. Output: bucket.

Each crank lifts the bucket a fraction of a turn. Add the turns and read the height.

Dials: numbers 2, representation 1, given 2, freedom 2, precision 2.

- 12 over 36. Measure 12. Part 36. Predict 1. Output bucket.
- 10 over 20. Measure 10. Part 20. Predict 2. Output bucket.
- 8 over 24. Measure 8. Part 24. Predict 2. Output bucket.

## Level 7: Well winch to bike

Job: Compound the ratio. Output: bucket.

Gear down 3 to 1, then compound to 12 to 1, and feel the push on the crank drop by the ratio.

Dials: numbers 3, representation 2, given 3, freedom 2, precision 3.

- 12, 36, then 48. Measure 12. Part 48. Predict 12. Output bucket.
- 8, 24, then 32. Measure 8. Part 32. Predict 12. Output bucket.
- 10, 30, then 40. Measure 10. Part 40. Predict 12. Output bucket.

## Level 8: Bicycle

Job: Roll the wheel. Output: wheel.

Each pedal turn rolls the wheel by its circumference. Compare two gears as a percent.

Dials: numbers 4, representation 2, given 3, freedom 2, precision 3.

- 12 to 24 teeth. Measure 12. Part 24. Predict 100. Output wheel.
- 12 to 18 teeth. Measure 12. Part 18. Predict 50. Output wheel.
- 10 to 20 teeth. Measure 10. Part 20. Predict 100. Output wheel.

## Level 9: Clock

Job: Turn the hands. Output: clock.

The minute hand turns 60 times for each hour. The hour hand turns at 12 to 1.

Dials: numbers 4, representation 3, given 4, freedom 2, precision 3.

- 12, 72, 10, 100. Measure 6. Part 100. Predict 60. Output clock.
- 10, 50, 12, 144. Measure 5. Part 144. Predict 30. Output clock.
- 8, 48, 10, 100. Measure 6. Part 100. Predict 12. Output clock.

## Level 10: Clock to hoist

Job: Hit the ratio. Output: bucket.

Solve for the tooth count that hits the target ratio and still fits on the board.

Dials: numbers 5, representation 3, given 4, freedom 3, precision 4.

- Unknown for 3 to 1. Measure 12. Part 36. Predict 36. Output bucket.
- Unknown for 4 to 1. Measure 12. Part 48. Predict 48. Output bucket.
- Unknown for 5 to 1. Measure 12. Part 60. Predict 60. Output bucket.

## Level 11: Hoist

Job: Place the axles. Output: bucket.

Set the axles off one line. The center spacing is half the two tooth counts added.

Dials: numbers 5, representation 3, given 5, freedom 3, precision 4.

- Spacing of 12 and 36. Measure 12. Part 36. Predict 24. Output bucket.
- Spacing of 8 and 24. Measure 8. Part 24. Predict 16. Output bucket.
- Spacing of 10 and 30. Measure 10. Part 30. Predict 20. Output bucket.

## Level 12: Hoist and watch

Job: Slow the hands. Output: clock.

Use the fewest gears that still give 60 to 1 for the minute hand and 12 to 1 for the hour hand.

Dials: numbers 6, representation 3, given 5, freedom 3, precision 5.

- Two stages to 60. Measure 6. Part 100. Predict 2. Output clock.
- Another two stages. Measure 5. Part 144. Predict 12. Output clock.
- 8, 48, 10, 100 again. Measure 6. Part 100. Predict 360. Output clock.

## Level 13: Crane hoist

Job: Lift under a budget. Output: hook.

Lift the 20 kg hook load. Power is torque times angular speed. Stay inside the size and part budget.

Dials: numbers 6, representation 3, given 6, freedom 4, precision 5.

- Torque in 4, ratio 4. Measure 12. Part 16. Predict 5. Output hook.
- Torque in 2, ratio 2. Measure 12. Part 4. Predict 10. Output hook.
- Torque in 3, ratio 3. Measure 12. Part 9. Predict 1. Output hook.
