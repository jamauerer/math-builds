The four operations are now the ladder steps (A1 to A18, S1 to S15, M1 to M13, D1 to D10). Grade 1 stays inside A13 and S11. Placement is plain notation. The sketch area is always on.

- `place-q1.png` — grade 1 placement question 1, `9 + 7 = ?` at A6. No words, no picture.
- `place-q5.png` — grade 1 placement question 5, `3 + 8 = ?` at A6.
- `place-q10.png` — grade 1 placement question 10, `50 - 40 = ?` at S7.
- `a12-carry.png` — A12 stacked `44 + 18` with the carry 1 written above the tens.
- `s11-borrow.png` — S11 stacked `53 - 49` with the tens crossed and 10 added to the ones.

- `m9-grade3.png` — grade 3 M9 stacked `94 × 5`.
- `sample-grade-1.md` — 60 questions as a grade 1 learner would see them.
