# Silent

The app makes no sound. Pre-reader questions are pictures. Words stay small for a parent. A hand shows the action once.

- `fill-box.png` — A1 add. A dotted empty box means fill it. Two piles of blocks, no Hear it.
- `count-tray.png` — count to 10. A numeral sits beside its dots and an empty tray.
- `compare-piles.png` — two piles, the goat pointing, tap the bigger one. Numerals sit next to their dots.
- `hand-demo.png` — the first-time hand mid-motion, moving a block toward the box.
