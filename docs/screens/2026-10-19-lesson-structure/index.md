# Lesson structure

19 Oct 2026. An Earn sitting is a lesson: warm-up, at most one new skill, then a mixed practice run.

- `warmup.png`: five questions from skills already in play. This one is the first.
- `learn.png`: a worked example for Divide to 100, steps in handwriting, one sentence of why. Gussets and bolts are already earned.
- `guided.png`: the first of three questions with the hint already open.
- `mixed.png`: a practice-run question from another strand while the bottom beam is the piece being earned.
- `skill-grid.png`: the parent view. Each strand shows its skills as new, learning, practicing, or mastered, with the last answers on the skill.
