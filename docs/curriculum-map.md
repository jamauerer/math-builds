# Curriculum map

Mastery of a skill level needs three records: the mapped challenge completed (Practiced), one transfer problem from the practice generators in the same sitting (Transferred), and one recall problem passed on a later day (Mastered). Completing a challenge unlocks the next challenge in that family at once. Mastered never locks the next challenge. Age does not open or lock a challenge.

Each new strand has at least three skills. A challenge is tagged to a skill whose generator is the operation the challenge asks for.

## Limits the models grade

- Every bridge scene stores a sag limit (span / 20 unless a scene sets another) and a stress limit of 100. A beam fails on whichever ratio is larger, and the result names that limit.
- The lever stores a plank torque limit of 120 N m. Balance fails when a side passes it.
- A compound gear train multiplies every stage. A clock scene also grades a 12 to 1 hour hand, and the hour hand in the test turns at that rate.

## Still not a perfect map

- Beam depth uses the cube in the sag formula, and the tagged skill is still multiplication.
- A pier halves the span. The tagged skill is still subtraction.
- Half-meter answers are typed in the workshop. The gap generator is still whole numbers.

## Skills with no machine challenge yet

These stay in the graph for placement and practice:

- The rest of place-value, addition-subtraction, and multiplication beyond the ids above.
- The extra skills in fractions, measurement, geometry, ratios, algebra, functions, statistics, and trigonometry that are not named in the table.
