# Choice

Choice skills open as large tappable cards. No keypad. One right option among plausible wrongs, shuffled.

- `plank-halves.png` — "Tap the plank cut in half" with three plank cards (2 equal, 3 equal, 2 unequal).
- `which-more.png` — "Which has more?" with two bolt groups.
- `tap-7.png` — "Tap 7" with four numeral cards.
- `angle.png` — "Is this angle right, acute or obtuse?" with three labeled buttons and the brace on the card.
