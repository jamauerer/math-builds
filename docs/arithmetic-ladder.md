# Math Builds arithmetic ladder

The exact step order for the four operations. Each step is one skill with fixed number ranges. A learner moves up one step at a time. Generators must stay inside the ranges written here; nothing outside them is ever served for that step.

Notation is always plain: `7 + 5 = ?`. No sentences, no pictures, except where a step says "dots".
"Stacked" means the problem is written in columns, one number above the other, with a line under it, so the learner can write carries and borrows on the sketch area.

## Rules

1. **Placement uses only these steps, in plain notation.** No word problems, no pictures in placement. It finds the step per operation, not a level number.
2. **A grade caps what can be served.** The highest step any question may come from is the grade's ceiling, plus one step: K: A4, S4. Grade 1: A13, S11. Grade 2: A16, S13, M1. Grade 3: A17, S14, M9, D6. Grade 4 and up: whole ladder. Other strands (fractions, geometry, algebra) do not appear at all before grade 3, and never above the grade's level in docs/curriculum.md.
3. **A run is mostly plain steps.** Plain questions from the ladder are at least 5 of every 6. One word problem per piece at most, and only from steps the learner has at Practicing or better: a word problem never introduces a step.
4. **Pictures only when the picture is the question.** Dots for A1, A2, S1, S2, M1, D1. Planks for fractions. Shapes for geometry. Nowhere else. A picture must pass the picture check (every drawn count and label matches the question); a picture that fails is not shown.
5. **The sketch area is always there.** A large drawable area beside or under every question, at least as big as the question card, pencil and eraser, cleared per question. Stacked problems are drawn into it with room above each column for carries and borrows.
