# Location

The profile asks where you live. Country sets default units and grade names; both stay switchable on their own.

- `country.png` — "Where do you live?" with a searchable country list (flag plus name), defaulting from the browser locale.
- `grade-de.png` — German profile grade pick: "Which Klasse?", metric units, "Using Klasse."
- `grade-us.png` — United States profile grade pick: "What grade are you in?", US units, "Using US grades."
