# lever ladder

## Level 1: Which side down

Job: See the seesaw. Output: kids.

Watch which kid goes down, then place the other kid so the seesaw balances.

Dials: numbers 0, representation 0, given 0, freedom 0, precision 0.

- 4 kg and 2 kg. Measure 1. Part 2. Predict 1. Output kids.
- 3 kg and 2 kg. Measure 2. Part 3. Predict 1. Output kids.
- 6 kg and 3 kg. Measure 1. Part 2. Predict 1. Output kids.

## Level 2: Move one kid

Job: Balance two kids. Output: kids.

Move one kid until the two kids balance on the seesaw.

Dials: numbers 1, representation 0, given 1, freedom 0, precision 0.

- 2 kg farther out. Measure 2. Part 1. Predict 1. Output kids.
- 4 kg and 2 kg. Measure 2. Part 4. Predict 1. Output kids.
- 3 kg and 1 kg. Measure 1. Part 3. Predict 1. Output kids.

## Level 3: Count the units

Job: Count along the plank. Output: kids.

Count the units of distance and seat the kids so the seesaw stays level.

Dials: numbers 1, representation 1, given 1, freedom 1, precision 1.

- 3 units each. Measure 3. Part 3. Predict 3. Output kids.
- 4 units each. Measure 4. Part 4. Predict 4. Output kids.
- 2 units each. Measure 2. Part 2. Predict 2. Output kids.

## Level 4: Multiply the moment

Job: Match the moments. Output: kids.

Mass times distance matches on both sides of the seesaw.

Dials: numbers 1, representation 1, given 2, freedom 1, precision 1.

- 4 kg at 2 meters. Measure 2. Part 4. Predict 8. Output kids.
- 3 kg at 3 meters. Measure 3. Part 3. Predict 9. Output kids.
- 5 kg at 2 meters. Measure 2. Part 2. Predict 10. Output kids.

## Level 5: Divide for distance

Job: Find the seat. Output: kids.

- 6 kg at 2 meters. Measure 2. Part 3. Predict 3. Output kids.
- 8 kg at 1 meter. Measure 1. Part 4. Predict 4. Output kids.

## Level 6: Fraction of a meter

## Level 7: Wheelbarrow

Job: Lift the load. Output: barrow.

The wheelbarrow's mechanical advantage is the effort arm divided by the load arm. Lift the stated load.

Dials: numbers 3, representation 2, given 3, freedom 2, precision 3.

- 12 kg, arms 1 and 2. Measure 1. Part 6. Predict 2. Output barrow.
- 8 kg, arms 1 and 2. Measure 1. Part 4. Predict 2. Output barrow.
- 15 kg, arms 2 and 6. Measure 2. Part 5. Predict 3. Output barrow.

## Level 8: Three loads

Job: Share three loads. Output: barrow.

Three loads sit in the wheelbarrow. The effort is a percent of the heaviest load.

Dials: numbers 4, representation 2, given 3, freedom 2, precision 3.

- Loads 10, 20, and 30. Measure 2. Part 5. Predict 20. Output barrow.
- Loads 8, 16, and 24. Measure 4. Part 5. Predict 16. Output barrow.
- Loads 12, 18, and 24. Measure 2. Part 4. Predict 18. Output barrow.

## Level 9: Unknown mass

- 6 kg at 1 meter. Measure 2. Part 3. Predict 3. Output kids.

## Level 10: Drawbridge weight

Job: Hold the leaf. Output: leaf.

The counterweight must be at least the safe mass, and the moments must match, or the leaf drops.

Dials: numbers 5, representation 3, given 4, freedom 3, precision 4.

- At least 6 kg. Measure 2. Part 6. Predict 6. Output leaf.
- At least 4 kg. Measure 2. Part 4. Predict 4. Output leaf.
- At least 12 kg. Measure 1. Part 12. Predict 12. Output leaf.

## Level 11: Tilt torque

Job: Hold the leaf as it tilts. Output: leaf.

As the drawbridge tilts, torque is mass times gravity times the arm. Gravity in this model is 10.

Dials: numbers 5, representation 3, given 5, freedom 3, precision 4.

- 3 kg at 2 meters. Measure 2. Part 2. Predict 60. Output leaf.
- 4 kg at 2 meters. Measure 2. Part 2. Predict 80. Output leaf.
- 2 kg at 3 meters. Measure 3. Part 3. Predict 60. Output leaf.

## Level 12: Torque and angle

Job: Read torque as the leaf moves. Output: leaf.

Torque changes with the horizontal arm as the drawbridge leaf rises. Balance the counterweight.

Dials: numbers 6, representation 3, given 5, freedom 3, precision 5.

- 4 kg at 1 meter. Measure 1. Part 2. Predict 40. Output leaf.
- 5 kg at 1 meter. Measure 1. Part 1. Predict 50. Output leaf.
- 7 kg at 1 meter. Measure 1. Part 1. Predict 70. Output leaf.

## Level 13: Crane boom

Job: Hold the hook. Output: boom.

The crane boom is two levers. Keep a safety margin: the effort must match the load after both stages.

Dials: numbers 6, representation 3, given 6, freedom 4, precision 5.

- 12 kg through two stages. Measure 2. Part 3. Predict 3. Output boom.
- 8 kg through two stages. Measure 2. Part 2. Predict 2. Output boom.
- 16 kg through two stages. Measure 2. Part 4. Predict 4. Output boom.
